Our Services

Specialised drone cleaning for every exterior surface — residential and commercial, across the Gold Coast and surrounds.

Commercial Buildings

Full facade washes for offices, apartment towers and retail buildings. Render, concrete, composite panel and painted surfaces are treated with a low-pressure soft wash that lifts mould, salt and traffic grime without stripping coatings or forcing water into joints.

  • ✓ No lift hire, scaffolding or road closures
  • ✓ Minimal disruption to tenants and trading hours
  • ✓ Scheduled maintenance programs available

Photo: commercial facade wash in progress

Photo: roof before and after soft washing

Roofs & Gutters

Tile, metal and membrane roofs cleaned without a single boot on the surface. Our soft-wash solution breaks down mould, moss and lichen at the root, so the result lasts far longer than a high-pressure blast — and your tiles stay intact.

  • ✓ Zero foot traffic — no cracked tiles or fall risk
  • ✓ Treats the cause, not just the surface stain
  • ✓ Safe for Colorbond, terracotta and cement tile

Solar Panels

Dust, salt spray and bird droppings quietly cut how much power your array produces. We rinse panels with purified water — no detergents, no abrasives, no one walking between your rows.

  • ✓ Purified water leaves no residue or streaking
  • ✓ No foot traffic on panels or roof sheeting
  • ✓ Suitable for homes and large commercial arrays

Photo: solar array rinse from above

Photo: high-rise glass being cleaned by drone

High-Rise Windows

Window banks and curtain walls washed and rinsed to a spot-free finish, floor by floor. Because the work happens from the air, there's no swing stage booking, no abseil crew and no footpath closure below.

  • ✓ Purified-water rinse dries without spotting
  • ✓ No anchor points or rigging on your building
  • ✓ Far quicker turnaround than rope access

House Washing

Render, brick, weatherboard and cladding cleaned gently from gutter line to ground. Ideal for two- and three-storey homes where the upper walls have never been properly washed because nobody wanted to get a ladder up there.

  • ✓ Safe on paint, render and older coatings
  • ✓ Reaches upper storeys and awkward elevations
  • ✓ Great before a sale, inspection or repaint

Photo: two-storey home mid-wash

Photo: industrial roof cleaning, aerial view

Warehouses & Sheds

Large-format roof and wall cleaning for industrial sites. Drones cover big spans quickly, which means less downtime for your operation and none of the paperwork that comes with sending people onto an industrial roof.

  • ✓ No work-at-height permits or roof access required
  • ✓ Large areas covered in a single visit
  • ✓ Work scheduled around your operating hours

Not sure if we can clean it?

If it's an exterior surface that's hard to reach, it's worth a call. Give us the address and a description and we'll tell you honestly whether drone cleaning is the right tool for the job.
