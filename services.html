Our services

Every service below is delivered by drone — which means no scaffolding, no boom lifts, nobody on your roof, and a result you can see from the street (and from the air).

Residential & commercial

Exterior building & facade washing

Render, brick, cladding, concrete and painted surfaces all collect grime, salt spray, mould and mildew — especially on the coast. Our drone applies a low-pressure soft wash that lifts it all away without the harsh blasting that damages surfaces and seals.

  • Safe on render, paint, cladding and seals
  • Removes mould, mildew, dirt and coastal salt build-up
  • No scaffolding or access equipment on your property
  • Ideal preparation before repainting or sale
Soft wash, not blast wash

Roof cleaning & soft washing

Walking on a roof cracks tiles, voids warranties and puts people at risk. Our drone treats tile, Colorbond and metal roofs from above with a gentle soft-wash solution that kills moss, lichen and mould at the source — so the roof stays cleaner for longer.

  • Zero foot traffic on fragile tiles
  • Treats moss, lichen and mould — not just the surface stain
  • Suits tile, Colorbond, metal and steep or complex rooflines
  • Before-and-after aerial photos included
Strata, hospitality & facilities

Multi-storey & commercial buildings

For strata blocks, hotels, aged-care facilities, clubs and commercial premises, traditional facade cleaning means weeks of scaffolding, road closures and disrupted residents or customers. A drone does the same job from the air — often in a fraction of the time and cost.

  • No scaffolding, swing stages or elevated work platforms
  • Minimal disruption to residents, guests and trading hours
  • Flexible scheduling, including early-morning starts
  • Great for buildings due for presentation upgrades
Protect your investment

Solar panel cleaning

Dust, salt, pollen and bird droppings quietly rob solar panels of output. Our drone gives them a gentle, low-pressure rinse — no one climbing on your roof, no abrasive brushes, no risk to the panels or their warranty.

  • Gentle clean that's safe for panels and mounting
  • No roof access or foot traffic required
  • Helps panels perform the way they were designed to
  • Easy to bundle with a roof or gutter clean
The finishing touches

Gutters, eaves & exterior surfaces

Grimy eaves, streaked fascia and dirty gutter lines drag down the look of an otherwise clean building. We wash the exterior surfaces that ladders make slow and risky — plus awnings, signage and other hard-to-reach details.

  • Eaves, fascia and external gutter faces
  • Awnings, carports and patio roofs
  • Signage and shopfront exteriors
  • Finishes off a facade or roof clean perfectly
