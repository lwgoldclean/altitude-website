About Altitude

A local Gold Coast business on a simple mission: make exterior cleaning safer, faster and kinder to the environment — by taking the people off the ladders and putting a drone in the sky instead.

Our story

Why we swapped ladders for flight controllers

Exterior cleaning hasn't changed much in decades: ladders, scaffolding, boom lifts and rope access. It's slow, it's expensive to set up, and it puts people at risk every single day — falls from height remain one of the most common causes of serious injury on Australian work sites.

Altitude Drone Exterior Cleaning was started to do it differently. Purpose-built spray drones can now deliver a professional soft wash to surfaces that used to take days of setup to reach — steep roofs, multi-storey facades, awkward rooflines — all flown by a pilot standing safely on the ground.

The result is a clean that's quicker, less disruptive and more sustainable, whether it's a family home in Burleigh or a multi-storey building in Southport.

What we stand for

The values behind every flight

Safety first

Every job is planned and risk-assessed before the drone leaves the ground, and our crew never works at heights. Full stop.

Quality you can see

We inspect our own work — from the ground and from the air — and we're not finished until you're happy with the result.

Sustainability

Low-pressure soft washing uses less water than blast cleaning, and we choose biodegradable solutions wherever the job allows.

Local & personal

We're Gold Coast locals. When you call, you talk to the person who'll actually do the job — not a call centre.

Safety & compliance

Professional operations, planned properly

Drone spraying isn't a gadget bolted onto a hose — it's a professional aviation operation. Before any flight we assess the site, weather, airspace and surroundings, establish an exclusion zone, and select the right treatment for each surface. Flights are conducted in line with Australian drone safety rules.
