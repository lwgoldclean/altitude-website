About

A specialist contractor, not a general cleaner

Gold Coast based, operating across South East Queensland and Northern New South Wales.

Why we exist

The exterior surfaces that most need maintenance are the ones that are hardest and most dangerous to reach. Historically the only answer has been to put a person up there — on a ladder, a rope, a scaffold or an elevated work platform — and manage the consequences with permits, harnesses and supervision.

That approach carries three costs an asset owner feels directly: the safety exposure, the price of the access equipment itself, and the disruption to the building while it is in place. On a commercial or government site, the access can easily cost more than the cleaning.

Altitude was established to deliver the same maintenance outcome without any of the three. The aircraft goes up; nobody else does. We are a single-discipline specialist — this is all we do, and we would rather tell you a site does not suit the method than take on work we cannot deliver well.

Equipment

The aircraft

A purpose-built heavy-lift spray platform — not a camera drone adapted for the task.

  • Six rotors, not four

    A hexacopter configuration carries motor redundancy. Loss of a single motor in flight leaves the aircraft controllable and recoverable — a material consideration when operating above an occupied building or a public thoroughfare.

  • Carbon fibre airframe

    Stiff and light, so lifting capacity goes into carrying solution rather than structure. Folding arms and props allow deployment into constrained side setbacks, courtyards and plant areas that an EWP cannot enter.

  • Pressurised spray system

    Nozzles deliver an even fan at a controlled rate and standoff distance. The cleaning is done by chemistry and dwell time rather than impact pressure, which is what makes the method safe on sealed joints, coatings and aged substrates.

  • Ground-based crew

    Remote pilot on the controls, dedicated observer maintaining watch on the aircraft and the ground beneath it. We do not operate single-crewed on commercial sites.

How we work

Fixed scope, fixed price

Pricing is agreed against a defined scope before mobilisation. No day-rate creep and no variations raised on site for work that was visible at scoping.

Direct accountability

The person who quotes your job is the person who flies it. Nothing is handed to a subcontractor you have not met and cannot hold to account.

Honest scoping

If a site is unsuitable, in restricted airspace, or better served by another trade, we say so before you commit — not after we have mobilised.

Evidence, not assurances

Every job closes with photographic evidence per elevation. You should not have to take a contractor's word for what happened three storeys up.

Governance

Safety and compliance you can evidence

Drone cleaning is aviation work carried out on a commercial site. It is governed by both Civil Aviation Safety Authority rules and Queensland work health and safety obligations, and we operate to both.

  • CASA-compliant operations

    Flights conducted under Civil Aviation Safety Authority regulations, with airspace assessed and authorisations obtained per site.

  • Trained & certified crew

    Every job flown by a certified remote pilot with a dedicated observer — never a single-person operation on a commercial site.

  • Documented site controls

    Site-specific SWMS, risk assessment, ground exclusion zones and stakeholder notification, issued before mobilisation for your records.

  • Insured operations

    Public liability and aviation cover maintained, with certificates of currency supplied on request or with tender responses.
