About Altitude

Gold Coast owned and operated, using drone technology to take people off ladders and roofs for good.

Professional Cleaning, Without the Risk

Exterior cleaning has always come with a trade-off: the surfaces that need it most are the hardest and most dangerous to reach. Ladders, scaffolding, boom lifts and rope access all add cost, time and risk to what should be a straightforward job.

Altitude Drone Exterior Cleaning was built to remove that trade-off. We use heavy-lift spray drones to apply soft-wash solution to roofs, facades, windows and solar panels — all controlled from the ground. Nobody climbs, nothing gets anchored to your building, and the job is finished in a fraction of the time.

We're a local business serving the Gold Coast and surrounds, and we treat every job as an aviation operation: planned, risk-assessed and documented. You get a fixed quote up front and a photo report when we're done.

Safety and Compliance You Can Trust

Drone cleaning is aviation work, and we treat it that way. Every flight is planned, risk-assessed and flown to Australian aviation standards.

  • CASA-Compliant Operations

    All flights conducted in line with Civil Aviation Safety Authority regulations, with airspace checks before every job.

  • Trained & Licensed Operators

    Your job is flown by a trained pilot with a dedicated spotter — never a one-person operation.

  • Site Safety Protocols

    Exclusion zones, neighbour notification and documented risk assessments on every single site.
