The commercial case

On most buildings, getting to the surface costs more than cleaning it

Scaffold hire, an elevated work platform, a swing stage or a rope crew can account for more of the invoice than the work itself — before you count the lost car parking, the traffic management, the tenant complaints and the weeks the equipment sits on site.

Drone delivery removes that entire line item. The crew arrives in a van, sets up on the ground, and the building is cleaned from the air. Most sites are finished in days rather than weeks, with the car park open and the tenants none the wiser.

Lower total cost

No access equipment to hire, transport, erect, certify or dismantle. That saving comes off your quote, not ours.

Days, not weeks

There is no mobilisation period. Work starts the morning we arrive and the site is clear when we leave.

Business as usual

No lost parking bays, no closed footpath, no obscured shopfront, no scaffold for tenants to look at for a month.

Risk off your books

Nobody works at height, so the fall hazard is removed rather than managed — the highest control available to you. Compliance detail →

Results

A better clean, and a gentler one

Cost and speed matter, but not if the result is poor or the building is damaged getting there. Soft washing is the method specifiers prefer for exactly that reason.

Treats the cause

Chemistry and dwell time break down mould, algae and lichen at the root. High pressure only removes what is visible, so it returns sooner.

Protects the substrate

Low pressure will not strip coatings, force water past seals into cladding cavities, or erode render and mortar joints.

No trafficking damage

Nobody walks the roof or the array, so no cracked tiles, deformed sheeting, punctured membrane or micro-fractured modules.

Longer intervals

Because the growth is treated rather than blasted off, the surface stays clean longer — which lowers the cost across the cycle, not just the visit.

Scopes we deliver

Single assets through to multi-site portfolios, delivered as one-off works or under a scheduled maintenance program.

Facade & cladding

Render, concrete, composite panel and painted surfaces. Low-pressure soft wash that lifts organic growth and salt without driving water into joints or stripping coatings.

Roof & gutter

Tile, metal and membrane roofs treated with no roof loading and no foot traffic — removing both the fall risk and the risk of trafficking damage to the sheet or membrane.

Windows & curtain wall

Purified-water cleaning of glazed elevations without swing stage mobilisation, rope crews, anchor point certification or footpath closure below.

Solar arrays

Soiling losses recovered across commercial rooftop and ground-mount arrays, with no module loading and no personnel traversing the array.

Industrial & warehouse

Large-span roof sheeting and wall cladding covered in a single mobilisation, scheduled around operating hours and without a roof access permit.

Scheduled programs

Recurring cyclical cleaning across a portfolio, with consolidated reporting and invoicing aligned to your maintenance calendar and budget cycle.
