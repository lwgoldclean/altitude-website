Why Altitude

Drone-powered washing that's faster, safer and smarter

Traditional exterior cleaning means scaffolding, boom lifts and people dangling from ropes. We replaced all of that with a precision spray drone — and a crew that never leaves the ground.

Faster results, less disruption

Drones can clean large exteriors up to five times faster than scaffold or rope-access crews, so your home or business gets back to normal sooner.

Unmatched safety

Nobody leaves the ground. No ladders, no scaffolding, no elevated work platforms — the risk of working at heights is designed out of the job entirely.

Eco-friendly soft washing

Low-pressure application with biodegradable solutions targets mould, mildew and grime at the source while minimising runoff and chemical use.

Reaches what others can't

Steep roofs, multi-storey facades, awkward rooflines and fragile surfaces — if you can see it from the ground, our drone can clean it.
