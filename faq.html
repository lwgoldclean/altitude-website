Frequently asked questions

Everything you've wondered about having your building washed by a drone. Can't find your answer? Give us a call — we're happy to talk it through.

How does drone exterior cleaning actually work?

A purpose-built spray drone is connected to a pump and tank system on the ground. Our pilot flies the drone along your roof or facade while it applies a low-pressure soft-wash treatment — a mix of water and cleaning solution matched to the surface. The drone hovers close to the surface for precise, even coverage, and the pilot controls everything from the ground with a live camera view.

Is it as effective as traditional pressure washing?

Yes — and for organic growth like mould, lichen and algae, soft washing is generally more effective. High-pressure blasting strips the visible layer but often leaves spores behind, so the growth returns quickly. A soft-wash treatment kills the growth at its source, which means the surface stays cleaner for longer and fragile materials aren't damaged in the process.

What buildings and surfaces can you clean?

Almost any exterior surface: tile, Colorbond and metal roofs, rendered and painted walls, brick, concrete, cladding, solar panels, eaves, fascia, gutter exteriors, awnings and signage. Drones are especially useful for surfaces that are steep, fragile, or expensive to reach — multi-storey facades, atriums, skylights and complex rooflines.

If you're unsure whether your property suits, send us the address and we'll tell you straight away.

How much does it cost?

Every property is different, so we quote each job individually — for free and with no obligation. What we can say is that for anything high or hard to reach, drone cleaning usually comes in well under traditional methods, because you're not paying for scaffolding, boom lift hire or rope-access crews. The quote we give you is the price you pay.

Is it safe?

Safety is the whole point. Nobody works at heights — the pilot and crew stay on the ground for the entire job. Before any flight we assess the site, weather and surroundings, set up an exclusion zone, and plan the flight path. Operations are conducted in line with Australian drone safety rules.

How long does a job take?

Much less time than you'd expect. Because there's no scaffolding to build or lifts to position, most residential jobs are done in hours, not days. Larger commercial buildings that would traditionally take weeks of setup and cleaning can often be completed up to five times faster by drone.

What cleaning solutions do you use?

We use professional soft-wash solutions applied at low pressure, matched to each surface and the type of build-up being treated. We choose biodegradable products wherever the job allows, use only as much as the surface needs, and take care around gardens, pets and waterways.

Can you reach areas other cleaners can't?

That's exactly where drones shine. Steep or fragile roofs that can't be walked on, facades above ladder height, tight spaces where a boom lift won't fit, buildings on sloping or landscaped sites — the drone simply flies to the surface. If you've been told an area is "too hard to access", we'd love to take a look.
