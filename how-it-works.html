What Happens on the Day

Every job runs as a planned flight operation. Here's what that looks like from your side of the fence.

  1. 1

    Site set-up and checks

    We park close to the building, set up pumps and water on the ground, and run airspace and weather checks before anything takes off.

  2. 2

    Exclusion zone and notifications

    A small ground exclusion zone is marked out below the work area, and neighbours or building management are notified in advance where it's needed.

  3. 3

    The wash

    The drone applies soft-wash solution in overlapping passes at a set distance from the surface. Dwell time does the cleaning, not pressure — so nothing is forced into seals or joints.

  4. 4

    Rinse, pack down and report

    A purified-water rinse finishes the job, we pack the site down completely, and your photo report lands the same day.

Common Questions

Do I need to be home? +

Not usually. As long as we have access to the property and an outdoor tap or space for our water tank, most jobs can go ahead while you're at work. We'll let you know beforehand if your site needs someone on hand.

Is drone cleaning safe for my roof and paint? +

Yes. Soft washing uses low pressure and a cleaning solution that does the work chemically, which is gentler than the high-pressure blasting many people are used to. There's also no weight or foot traffic on the surface at any point.

What about my plants, pets and neighbours? +

Our solutions are biodegradable, and we pre-wet and rinse garden beds around the work area as a precaution. Pets are best kept indoors during the flight, and we notify immediate neighbours where the work is close to a boundary.

What happens if the weather turns? +

Wind and rain both affect flight safety and cleaning quality, so if conditions aren't right we'll reschedule at no cost to you. We'd rather move a booking than fly a job we can't do properly.

How long does a job take? +

Most homes are done within a few hours. Larger commercial buildings depend on height and surface area — we'll give you a realistic time estimate with your quote so you can plan around it.
