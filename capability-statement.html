1. Overview

Altitude Drone Exterior Cleaning is a specialist drone services contractor based on the Gold Coast, delivering exterior cleaning and maintenance to commercial, government and strata clients across South East Queensland and Northern New South Wales.

We use heavy-lift spray aircraft, operated from ground level by a two-person crew, to clean facades, roofs, glazed elevations and solar arrays. The method removes the need for scaffold, elevated work platforms, rope access and roof entry — eliminating the fall-from-height hazard from the task rather than controlling it.

2. Client outcomes

Risk elimination

No personnel at height. The highest control in the WHS hierarchy, applied to a task that is normally managed at the lowest.

Reduced cost of access

No scaffold hire, EWP hire, swing stage mobilisation, anchor point certification or traffic management.

Minimal disruption

No loss of car parking, footpath, trading frontage or tenancy amenity for the duration of the works.

Asset protection

Low-pressure application avoids the coating damage, seal failure and water ingress associated with high-pressure cleaning.

Documented delivery

SWMS before mobilisation, photographic completion report per elevation, condition observations for asset records.

Program capability

Single sites through to multi-site portfolios on a scheduled cyclical program with consolidated reporting.

6. Delivery method

  1. 01Scope and assessment. Elevations, access, airspace and site sensitivities assessed. Most sites scoped from aerial imagery and drawings without an attendance fee.
  2. 02Proposal and documentation. Fixed-price scope issued with methodology, SWMS, insurance certificates and program.
  3. 03Mobilisation. Ground equipment established, exclusion zone set, stakeholders and neighbouring occupants notified.
  4. 04Delivery. Two-person crew — remote pilot and observer. Soft-wash application at controlled standoff, followed by rinse.
  5. 05Completion. Photographic report per elevation, condition observations and sign-off issued for asset records.
